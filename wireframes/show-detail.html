Soraya Vivian

Host & Creator, Pride This Way

Soraya Vivian is a dynamic storyteller, traveler, and advocate for LGBTQ+ visibility and rights. As the creator and host of Pride This Way, she brings her infectious energy and deep cultural curiosity to every episode, connecting with communities around the world.

With a background in digital media and a passion for amplifying underrepresented voices, Soraya joined Pillars of Change Media to build a show that goes beyond surface-level celebration — diving into the history, politics, and personal stories behind Pride movements everywhere.

When she's not on the road filming, Soraya can be found working with LGBTQ+ youth organizations, speaking at media conferences, and developing the next season of Pride This Way.
