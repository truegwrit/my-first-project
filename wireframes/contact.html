FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How does the philanthropy model work? +
A percentage of every dollar we earn from sponsorships, partnerships, and direct revenue is donated to nonprofits and communities aligned with our five pillars. We partner primarily with UPI Loan Fund, a certified CDFI, to ensure funds reach underserved communities effectively.
How can I sponsor a show? +
We partner with purpose-driven brands that align with our mission and values. Reach out to partners@pillarsofchange.com with information about your brand and which shows or pillars interest you, and our team will follow up.
Can I pitch a show or story idea? +
Absolutely. We're always looking for new voices and perspectives. Please send your pitch, a brief synopsis, and your bio to hello@pillarsofchange.com. We review all submissions and aim to respond within 4 weeks.
Where can I watch and listen to your content? +
Our shows are available on YouTube, Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Amazon Music, and wherever you listen to podcasts. Visit our Shows page for direct links to each program and platform.
How is Pillars of Change different from other media companies? +
We integrate philanthropy directly into our business model. Unlike traditional media, every piece of content we produce generates funding for communities in need. We don't just tell stories about change — we fund it.
