About Us

We Believe Stories Can Change the World

Pillars of Change Media is a purpose-driven content studio founded on the belief that powerful storytelling can create measurable social impact and amplify the voices that need to be heard most.

Founder / Company Photo

Our Story

Built on Purpose, Driven by Impact

Pillars of Change Media was founded by H.J. Taylor with a clear vision: to build a media company where every piece of content serves a greater purpose. From our headquarters in New York, we produce podcasts, vodcasts, digital experiences, immersive experiences, and digital applications that don't just entertain — they activate.

"We don't just tell stories. We turn audience engagement into advocacy and funding for the communities that need it most."

Our integrated philanthropy model means that a percentage of every dollar we earn goes directly to nonprofits and communities aligned with our mission. We partner with organizations like the UPI Loan Fund, a Community Development Financial Institution (CDFI), to ensure our impact is both tangible and lasting.

Our Foundation

The Five Pillars of Change

Every project, partnership, and piece of content we create is guided by these five pillars. They are our compass for driving real-world impact.

Icon

Human Rights

We believe in the inherent dignity and equal rights of every person. Our content highlights stories of resilience, resistance, and the ongoing fight for basic freedoms — from LGBTQ+ rights around the globe to refugee experiences and beyond. Through programming like "Pride This Way," we bring visibility to communities whose stories demand to be told.

Icon

Social Justice

Systemic inequities don't dismantle themselves. Our social justice pillar drives content that investigates and exposes failures in our institutions while highlighting the changemakers working to fix them. "Court on Trial" is our flagship social justice program, examining how the legal system impacts marginalized communities.

Icon

Education

Access to quality education is a fundamental right, yet millions are denied it. We create content that expands access to knowledge, highlights innovative educational models, and supports lifelong learning initiatives in underserved communities.

Icon

Health & Wellness

True wellness encompasses physical, mental, and emotional health. Our programming promotes holistic well-being, challenges health disparities, and advocates for equitable access to healthcare resources in communities that have historically been underserved.

Icon

Food Insecurity

Hunger is not a scarcity problem — it's a distribution and equity problem. We address food insecurity through storytelling that exposes the root causes, uplifts community-driven solutions, and channels funding toward building sustainable food systems for those who need them most.

Flagship Partnership

UPI Loan Fund (CDFI)

Our partnership with the UPI Loan Fund, a certified Community Development Financial Institution, is central to how we translate media revenue into tangible community impact.

CDFIs like UPI provide affordable lending, financial services, and development support to underserved communities that traditional financial institutions often overlook. By channeling a portion of our profits through UPI, we ensure that the communities represented in our storytelling receive direct economic benefit.

Our Journey

Milestones

From concept to launch, here's how Pillars of Change Media has grown into a purpose-driven content studio.

2024

The Vision Takes Shape

H.J. Taylor conceives Pillars of Change Media, outlining the five pillars and the integrated philanthropy model that would become the company's foundation.

2025

Pillars of Change, LLC Founded

The company is officially established in New York. Key team members including Mandy Goldberg join to build the content studio's production infrastructure.

2025

UPI Loan Fund Partnership

Pillars of Change forms its flagship nonprofit partnership with UPI Loan Fund, a certified CDFI, creating a direct pipeline from media revenue to community investment.

2026

Launch Programming Premieres

"Pride This Way" with Soraya Vivian and "Court on Trial" with Cris Wright debut as the studio's inaugural shows, covering LGBTQ+ culture and justice reform respectively.

2026 & Beyond

Expanding the Impact

New programming across all five pillars, expansion into immersive and digital experiences, and deeper community partnerships to amplify underrepresented voices worldwide.
